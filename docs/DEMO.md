# RescueLoop — shared application demonstration

Prepare separate donor and receiver accounts in two browser profiles. Save recovery codes privately and keep them out of recordings. Publish both directory profiles, configure compatible storage and receiving hours, and use a clearly identified synthetic batch with a pickup window that is currently open. These are demo records, not evidence of real food rescue or campus affiliation.

## 0:00–0:25 · The problem and accounts

“Dining teams and pantries need a reliable way to coordinate good surplus food before its pickup window closes. RescueLoop connects independent organizations and records the handoff.” Show the donor's overview and receiving partner's profile. Explain that these are separate email/password accounts sharing server records.

## 0:25–0:55 · Partners and constraints

Have the receiver find the donor in Partners, send a partnership request, and have the donor accept it. Show the new notification. Explain the receiver's daily capacity, storage, dietary restrictions, time zone, and receiving hours.

## 0:55–1:35 · Verified surplus and explainable matching

The donor enters a batch manually or structures a sample note. Identify whether the selected extractor is the offline parser or a configured IBM model. Save uncertain facts in review. Verify the handling and declared allergens only for the synthetic demonstration.

Switch to the receiver account, open the shared listing, and show the eligibility reasons: accepted partnership, verified listing, compatible food and storage, at least 15 minutes of receiving overlap, and enough daily capacity. Unknown allergens or unmet constraints block requests.

## 1:35–2:15 · Request, approve, coordinate

Request the pickup as the receiver. Show the notification as the donor, approve the request, and open the pickup conversation. Send a synthetic arrival message. Approval reserves capacity but does not increase rescued-food impact. Concurrent approvals use a database guard against overbooking.

## 2:15–3:00 · Collection and evidence

Once the agreed window has started, confirm the full collection as the receiver. Show the receipt and impact report in both accounts. A 20 kg batch contributes 20 rescued kg and 40 estimated meal equivalents under the displayed 0.5 kg assumption. Download the collection CSV.

“This proves the account-to-partner-to-pickup loop. Our next pilot work is validating campus handling procedures, affiliations, and measurement assumptions. Email delivery, verified email, and live routing are future additions.”

## Quick offline alternative

Use `/demo` when a network or account setup is impractical. Its inventory, partners, distances, and historical impact are fictional, stored in that browser, and use a fixed sample clock. Reset through Workspace settings before judging. Do not describe its records as shared production data or real-world impact.
